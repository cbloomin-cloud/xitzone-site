05About
About · Performance Coach

Always studying.
Always chasing.

I spent years as an athlete chasing performance. Somewhere along the way I realized the real work wasn't just in the training. It was in understanding how we actually perform when it matters.

These days, I help people and organizations close the gap between potential and execution.

Performance is my obsession. Not just in the gym, not just on the field, but in every environment where people are asked to show up and deliver.

Based in Colorado Springs, I live at the intersection of strength, cognitive science and real-world wellness, working with brands, communities and leaders who want more out of themselves and the people around them.

If any of that resonates, let's connect. I'm always up for a good conversation about performance, well-being or what's next.
