How it works

The 8 weeks

Weeks 1-2

Learn

Longer rest, learn every movement.

Weeks 3-4

Build

More work, less rest, up to the full 60/30 format.

Weeks 5-7

Double up

Two rounds per part at shorter intervals.

Week 8

Deload + test

One easy pass, then a push-up and wall-sit test.

RPE is how hard a set feels from 1 to 10. RPE 7 means you could do about 3 more good reps; RPE 8 means about 2. RIR means reps in reserve, the same idea counted in reps. Pick loads by feel, not by a number someone else lifts.

Coach's notes

Make it work

  • Every jump has a no-jump option: step instead of hop. Same muscles, less impact.
  • Live in an apartment? Low-impact options keep the neighbors happy too.
  • Warm-up from the original session: jumping jacks, twist and pivot, knee pulls, pigeon pulls, alternating quad stretch.
