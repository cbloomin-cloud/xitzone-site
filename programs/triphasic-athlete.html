How it works

The 8 weeks

Weeks 1-2

Eccentric

Lower every main rep for 5 seconds. Builds control and tendon strength.

Weeks 3-4

Isometric

Pause 3 seconds at the bottom. Builds strength in the hardest position.

Weeks 5-6

Concentric

Lower normally, drive up as fast as possible.

Week 7

Realization

Heavy sets paired with jumps (contrast).

Week 8

Deload + test

Light lifting, then test jumps and sprints.

RPE is how hard a set feels from 1 to 10. RPE 7 means you could do about 3 more good reps; RPE 8 means about 2. RIR means reps in reserve, the same idea counted in reps. Pick loads by feel, not by a number someone else lifts.

Coach's notes

Make it work

  • Youth athletes: technique first. Keep main lifts at RPE 7 or below with a qualified coach present.
  • Slow lowering is hard. Use about 10-15% less weight than normal in weeks 1-4.
  • Want the neuro side too (vision, balance, breath, focus)? That's WARP. See below.
