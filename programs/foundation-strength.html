How it works

The 8 weeks

Weeks 1-2

Learn the lifts

Moderate weights, 10 reps, own every rep.

Weeks 3-4

Build

More sets, 8 reps, a little heavier.

Weeks 5-7

Strength

Fewer reps, heavier weights, still clean form.

Week 8

Deload + test

Light week, then an optional 5-rep best on two lifts.

RPE is how hard a set feels from 1 to 10. RPE 7 means you could do about 3 more good reps; RPE 8 means about 2. RIR means reps in reserve, the same idea counted in reps. Pick loads by feel, not by a number someone else lifts.

Coach's notes

Make it work

  • Rest 2-3 minutes on main lifts and 60-90 seconds on accessories.
  • Double progression: hit the top of a rep range for every set, then add the smallest weight jump.
  • Days 3, 6 and 7 are recovery. Walk, sleep and eat protein.
