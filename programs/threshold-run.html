How it works

The 8 weeks

Weeks 1-3

Base

Short threshold reps, easy volume, hills for strength.

Weeks 4-6

Extend

Longer threshold reps and a longer long run.

Week 7

Sharpen

Continuous threshold run.

Week 8

Deload + test

Easy week ending in a 30-minute time trial or 5K.

RPE is how hard a set feels from 1 to 10. RPE 7 means you could do about 3 more good reps; RPE 8 means about 2. RIR means reps in reserve, the same idea counted in reps. Pick loads by feel, not by a number someone else lifts.

Coach's notes

Make it work

  • New to running? Cut every time by a third and use run-walk intervals.
  • 80% of your running should feel easy. The hard days only work if the easy days stay easy.
  • Pair with two short strength sessions per week (Foundation Strength Days 1 and 2 work well).
