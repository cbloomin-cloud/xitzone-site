How it works

The 8 weeks

Weeks 1-2

Learn the format

12-minute EMOMs and one Tabata.

Weeks 3-4

Build

16-minute EMOMs; add a second Tabata.

Weeks 5-7

Push

20-minute EMOMs and more reps.

Week 8

Deload + test

Short week, then a max-rep Tabata test.

RPE is how hard a set feels from 1 to 10. RPE 7 means you could do about 3 more good reps; RPE 8 means about 2. RIR means reps in reserve, the same idea counted in reps. Pick loads by feel, not by a number someone else lifts.

Coach's notes

Make it work

  • If you can't start the next minute on time, cut reps, not form.
  • Three days a week with a day off between is plenty. Add walking on other days.
  • Great as a group class: everyone works on the same clock at their own level.
