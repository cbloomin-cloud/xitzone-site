My specialty

W.A.R.P.

The Warrior Athlete Resilience Protocol · Powered by Neuro Functional Training

Athletes are more connected than ever, and more disconnected from their own bodies. Hours on screens limit how the eyes, balance system and movement work together: slower reactions, weaker coordination, scattered focus.

WARP trains the nervous system, not just the muscles. It blends neuro functional training with sports psychology, physiology and mindfulness to build athletes who are strong in body, sharp in mind and calm under pressure.

VestibularSpinning and balance drills for control in fast, chaotic movement.
Visual / VOREye-tracking work for faster reactions and game awareness.
Triphasic + kineticControlled unpredictability for reactive strength.
Breath + mindsetRegulate energy, focus and composure on demand.
6 weeks · one theme each week
1 Nervous system regulation2 Flow state + focus3 Sleep + recovery4 Stress management5 Performance mindset6 Full integration

A daily 5-minute neuro routine, weekly live sessions, app-based programming and pre- and post-testing. Because it's coached personally, WARP isn't a free download. Reach out and let's talk about you or your team.

About · Performance Coach

Always studying.
Always chasing.

I spent years as an athlete chasing performance. Somewhere along the way I realized the real work wasn't just in the training. It was in understanding how we actually perform when it matters.

These days, I help people and organizations close the gap between potential and execution.

Performance is my obsession. Not just in the gym, not just on the field, but in every environment where people are asked to show up and deliver.

Based in Colorado Springs, I live at the intersection of strength, cognitive science and real-world wellness, working with brands, communities and leaders who want more out of themselves and the people around them.

If any of that resonates, let's connect. I'm always up for a good conversation about performance, well-being or what's next.
