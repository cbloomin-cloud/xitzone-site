My story

Knocked down.
Came back
stronger.

I didn't always get to choose when I left my comfort zone. Twice, my own health made that choice for me. Both times I had to decide who I'd be on the other side.

I came back better, stronger, smarter and more grounded than before. Records, an All-American season and a two-sport college career came out of that comeback. More than any of that, I came out with the lesson I now coach: growth needs balance to last.

The approach

the edge.

Pushing harder only works for so long. Real progress happens when all three parts of you move forward together.

Mind

Focus, mindset and the habits behind them. Learn to stay clear and steady when life gets loud, and to choose discomfort on purpose.

Training, movement and recovery built around your life, not the other way around. Strong, mobile and resilient for the long run.

Stress, setbacks and the stories we tell ourselves. Build the emotional ground that lets you bounce back instead of break down.
