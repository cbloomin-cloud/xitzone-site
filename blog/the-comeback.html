The comeback.

By Caydrick · 5 min read

Most people get to decide when they step out of their comfort zone. I didn't. Twice, my own body made that decision for me. Both times, the only choice left was how I'd respond.

Twelve

I was diagnosed with Crohn's disease at 12 years old. At an age when most kids are just figuring out what sport they love, I was learning words like "flare-up" and "inflammation" and trying to understand what this would mean for the rest of my life.

I decided it wouldn't mean less. I kept training. By the end of high school I held the school record in the pole vault and was named a McDonald's All American nominee in basketball. Crohn's was part of my story, but it wasn't going to write the ending.

Nineteen

Then, at 19, it caught up with me. Complications from Crohn's put me in the hospital, and I went through multiple life-saving surgeries. Everything I'd built as an athlete suddenly felt very far away. This exit from my comfort zone wasn't a challenge I'd signed up for. It was survival.

You don't always choose the exit. You always choose what you do next.

The way back

Recovery wasn't a straight line. It meant rebuilding my body, but also my mind and my emotions: learning patience, trusting the process, and letting go of the version of me that existed before.

I got back on track. I became a two-sport college athlete in basketball and track, earned All-American honors, and set a school record in college. I came out the other side better, stronger, smarter, and more grounded in life than ever.

What it taught me

Pushing hard got me far, but it isn't what brought me back. Balance did. Taking care of my mind, my body and my emotions together, not just grinding through one of them, is what made my comeback last.

That's what Xitzone is about. Exiting your comfort zone isn't about pushing harder until something breaks. It's about choosing growth on purpose, even when life forces the exit for you, and building the balance that lets you come out stronger.

Wherever you're starting from, you can come back too.
